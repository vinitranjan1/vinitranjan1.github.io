I am a postdoctoral researcher in [Bartolomeo Stellato's group](https://stellato.io/) at Princeton's [Operations Research and Financial Engineering (ORFE)](https://orfe.princeton.edu/) department, where I completed my PhD.

My research focuses on *efficient algorithms for real-time optimization*. Specfically, I work on theoretical and data-driven methods to verify performance and design algorithms for parametric problems.

I completed my undergraduate degree in Math and Computer Science from Duke University (December 2019).

My email is vranjan AT princeton DOT edu.

Starting in Fall 2026, I will be joining [Haihao Lu's group](https://mitsloan.mit.edu/faculty/directory/haihao-lu) in MIT Sloan's [Operations Research and Statistics](https://mitsloan.mit.edu/faculty/academic-groups/operations-research-statistics/about-us) group as a postdoctoral researcher.
I will be working on *Scalable GPU-Accelerated Optimization Methods*.
