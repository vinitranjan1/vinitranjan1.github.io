I am a postdoctoral associate in [Haihao Lu's group](https://mitsloan.mit.edu/faculty/directory/haihao-lu) at the MIT Sloan School of Management in the [Operations Research and Statistics](https://mitsloan.mit.edu/faculty/academic-groups/operations-research-statistics/about-us) group. I work on scalable, GPU-accelerated optimization methods.

I completed my PhD in [Bartolomeo Stellato's group](https://stellato.io/) at Princeton's [Operations Research and Financial Engineering (ORFE)](https://orfe.princeton.edu/) department. I worked on theoretical and data-driven methods to verify performance and design efficient algorithms for parametric problems in real-time optimization.

I completed my undergraduate degree in Math and Computer Science from Duke University (December 2019).
